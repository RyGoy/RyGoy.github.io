# It all comes down to accounting

[The accounting lens](_index.md) · step 1 of 8 · Next: [Every entry has two sides](02-every-entry-has-two-sides.md)

## The idea

Most people read a court case, a bill or a bank statement as a matter of rules: who is allowed to do what. The archive asks you to read every one of them as a matter of [**accounting**](../terms/accounting.md): who owes what to whom, and who is responsible when it isn't paid. In Scott Duncan's words, "[LEGAL always boils down to ‘who pays, and how much do they owe’ (SURETY and ACCOUNTING)](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/or-why-piece-of-shit-chistiansare-such-pieces-of-shit/)".

He puts the whole archive in one line: "[LEGAL = SURETY AND ACCOUNTING.](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_xii/462493170453287_888758267826773/#entry-888769497825650)" [**Surety**](../terms/surety.md) "[simply means “responsible party”](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_v/462493170453287_606995862669683/)" ([step 5](05-surety-the-responsible-party.md)). In Chris Schulte's words, "[ACCOUNTING is the MECHANISM by which the story of the transferance of SURETY, is told.](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/various-authors/hero-journey/#:~:text=ACCOUNTING%20is%20the%20MECHANISM%20by%20which%20the%20story%20of%20the%20transferance%20of%20SURETY%2C%20is%20told.)" And, as Scott Duncan says, "[everything LEGAL has a monetary value](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/agency-for-idiots/)". Until you can see that story behind the words, the posts in the volumes will read like noise to the unlearned. Once you see the world in terms of **debits** and **credits**, they read like entries.

This path teaches the record first (steps 2 and 3), then what it records: title (4), surety (5), money (6), trust (7) and tender (8).

## In their words

> "SPOILERS: IT'S ALWAYS ABOUT ACCOUNTING AND SURETY."
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 5, 2 February 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_v/462493170453287_620036564698946/)

> "It all comes down to money. What you know as "law" is the by-product of money. LEGAL = SURETY and ACCOUNTING, the rules of which are TENDERED through that ACCOUNTING."
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 13, 7 March 2016](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_xiii/462493170453287_955376827831583/)

> "LEGAL = SURETY AND ACCOUNTING. … It doesn't matter which jurisdiction. If it doesn't fit into THAT SPECIFIC MODEL, then IT'S WRONG."
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 12, 6 October 2015](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_xii/462493170453287_888758267826773/#entry-888769297825670)

Chris Schulte, who wrote the archive's articles on accounting, says what the accounting is for:

> "The accounting is how you follow the money. In the banking part of the legal matrix, money flows through debits and credits."
> — [Chris Schulte, Who Does The World Owe 53 Trillion Dollars To?](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/chris-schulte/who-does-the-world-owe-53-trillion-dollars-to/)

> "It means that the people who's signature is on the money want to check and make sure that everything ADDS UP....that is what accountants do"
> — [Chris Schulte, An Article About Accounting](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/chris-schulte/an-article-about-accounting/)

## Check yourself

> [!question]- Scott Duncan says LEGAL = SURETY AND ACCOUNTING. What two questions does that tell you to ask of anything "legal"?
> Who is responsible (surety)? And what is owed, to whom, as the books record it (accounting)?
