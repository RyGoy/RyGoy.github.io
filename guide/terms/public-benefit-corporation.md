# Public Benefit Corporation

## Definitions

> A public benefit corporation (PBC) is a for-profit corporation created to operate in a responsible and sustainable manner while also pursuing one or more specified public benefits, in addition to generating profit for shareholders. Unlike traditional C corporations, which are primarily obligated to maximize shareholder value, PBCs are permitted, and required, to balance three considerations: Many states, including Delaware, authorize the formation of PBCs and require periodic public benefit reports assessing the corporation’s progress towards its stated public purpose.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/public_benefit_corporation) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
