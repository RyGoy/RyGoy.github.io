# Rule

## Definitions

> A rule is a standard, principle, or directive used to govern conduct, procedure, or decision-making. Its source and legal effect depend on context. A rule may come from a constitution, statute, court decision, court rule, administrative agency, contract, or institution, and the term does not on its own indicate whether a rule is legally binding.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/rule) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
