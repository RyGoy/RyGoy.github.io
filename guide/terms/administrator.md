# Administrator

## Definitions

> An ADMINISTRATOR by its very nature IS a TRUSTEE. That's what gives them the RIGHT to administer.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 8, Nov 1, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_viii/462493170453287_740119249357343/#entry-741301482572453)
