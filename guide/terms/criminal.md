# Criminal

## Definitions

> Criminal is a term used for a person who has committed a crime or has been legally convicted of a crime.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/criminal) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
