# Remittance

**Taught in:** [Tender, and discharge](../lens/08-tender-and-discharge.md)

## Definitions

> Remittance =  A Note in leu of PAYMENT, which ( as Derek Moran will happily confirm) is currently ILLEGAL.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Jan 21, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_468545096514761/#entry-468558269846777)

> That is the definition of REMITTANCE. ADVICE ON HOW TO DISCHARGE. Only the SURETY has the AUTHORITY to do that.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Aug 3, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_495437137158890/#entry-544514452251158)

> " REMITTANCE" = Instructions for how you WISH to discharge the account.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 4, Oct 7, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_iv/462493170453287_568338833202053/#entry-568627239839879)

> REMITTANCE=ADVICE FOR PAYMENT
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 7, Jul 10, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_vii/462493170453287_691324030903532/#entry-692149094154359)
