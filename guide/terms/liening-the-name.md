# Liening the Name

## Definitions

> Liening the NAME means the CORPORATION is presumed to have the value, not the name. The name OWES the corporation, so the name is responsible for the DEBT to the corporation, and nothing else.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 2, Apr 18, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_ii/462493170453287_498809460154991/#entry-504169786285625)

> LIENING YOUR NAME means you owe NOTHING. If you don't they can attach your name to ANYONE.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 3, Sep 3, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_iii/462493170453287_555874271115176/#entry-556110554424881)
