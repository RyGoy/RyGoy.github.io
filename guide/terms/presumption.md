# Presumption

## Definitions

> A presumption is an inference the law requires when certain facts are present. Most presumptions are rebuttable, meaning they can be overturned if the opposing evidence is strong enough. Others are conclusive and must be accepted as true, even if contrary evidence exists.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/presumption) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
