# Breach

## Definitions

> A breach is a violation of law or when a party fails to perform their part of a contractual agreement. For more information, see breach of contract.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/breach) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
