# Regulation

## Definitions

> A regulation is a rule made and maintained by an authority, typically a governmental agency, to control or govern conduct within its jurisdiction.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/regulation) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
