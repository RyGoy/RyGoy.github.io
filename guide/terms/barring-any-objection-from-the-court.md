# Barring Any Objection from the Court

## Definitions

> "Barring any objection from the court" = "UNLESS THE COURT OBJECTS".
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Nov 5, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_485010274868243/#entry-583579285011341)

## In Scott Duncan's words

He uses it in 1 posts, comments and articles. Where he says what it means, or uses it most plainly:

> "Barring any objection from the court" = "UNLESS THE COURT OBJECTS".
>
> The court is not a person, and/or party, so they CAN'T object.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, 5 November 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_485010274868243/#entry-583579285011341)
