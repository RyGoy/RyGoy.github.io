# Record

## Definitions

> Record has multiple legal definitions: 1) To file a copy of a real estate instrument which affects a parcel of real property’s title, such as a deed, with the land records office (often called the county recorder, registry of deeds, or something similar) for the county in which the real property is located. For reference, California Civil Code §§ 27279–27297.6 lays out the process for recording deeds and real estate documents.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/record) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
