# Liened

## Definitions

> My LEGAL name is LIENED. This means the SECURED PARTY has FIRST RIGHT OF REFUSAL to ANY CLAIM MADE ON THAT NAME.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 12, Dec 11, 2015](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_xii/462493170453287_912484872120779/#entry-913619428673990)
