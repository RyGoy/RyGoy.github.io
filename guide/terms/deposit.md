# Deposit

## Definitions

> You seem to think DEPOSITS are "in" an account, and forget that it is JUST accounting.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 3, Jul 5, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_iii/462493170453287_532494196786517/#entry-533315953371008)

## In Scott Duncan's words

He uses it in 10 posts, comments and articles. Where he says what it means, or uses it most plainly:

> YES! That's INCOME. If you have a record of having been PAID "income" (your employer) but no record of a DEPOSIT then it's TAXABLE.
>
> If you write FOR DEPOSIT ONLY in the "notes/memo" line of the cheque, then it is NOT taxable. It becomes...a DEPOSIT.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, 9 April 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_462528253783112/#entry-501138029922134)

> The jurisdiction is even narrower than you think. Government ONLY has claim to INCOME. INCOME is pay from "Employment" or gains through Money of Account.
>
> DEPOSITS are not part of the government's jurisdiction either. Jut put FOR DEPOSIT ONLY in the memo section of any cheques you deposit. NO INCOME TAX. Deposits AREN'T INCOME.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 2, 4 April 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_ii/462493170453287_499503200085617/#entry-499535006749103)

> Just as a note: You need only demonstrate INTENT in your instruction.
