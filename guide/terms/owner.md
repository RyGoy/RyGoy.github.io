# Owner

**Taught in:** [Title is all that is sold](../lens/04-title-is-all-that-is-sold.md)

## Definitions

> An owner is an individual who has the right to use, possess, and transfer something. An individual can be an owner of real property, personal property, or intellectual property. Proof of ownership is often vested in a title.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/owner) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
