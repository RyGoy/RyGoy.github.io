# Levy

## Definitions

> A levy may be a fine or tax imposed by a government authority. In this case, levy can also be used as a verb, as in “to levy taxes” which means to impose a tax.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/levy) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
