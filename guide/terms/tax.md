# Tax

## Definitions

> Tax is any charge of money or property that is imposed by a government upon individuals or entities that are within the government's authority to collect. This term, however, generally does not include charges imposed in exchange for the provision of specific goods or services (e.g., bridge tolls or sanitation fees).
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/tax) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
