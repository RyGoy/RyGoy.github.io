# Proper Notice

## Definitions

> Identifying WHO you are when giving a notice creates what’s called “PROPER NOTICE”.
> — [PROPER NOTICE FOR IDIOTS, May 5, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/proper-notice-for-idiots/)

> Proper Notice is simple: If somebody signed a legal document with your name on it, you NOTIFY them. Period.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 7, Aug 14, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_vii/462493170453287_706971612672107/#entry-707012712667997)
