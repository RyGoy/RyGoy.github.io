# Lien

**Taught in:** [Title is all that is sold](../lens/04-title-is-all-that-is-sold.md)

## Definitions

> Liening your name **creates a PUBLIC RECORD of an undisputed CLAIM**.
> — [LIEN YOUR NAME, May 5, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/lien-your-name/)

> A lien is your PUBLIC CLAIM to that BILL OF LADING, which means the government cannot use it.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 2, May 22, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_ii/462493170453287_515596301809640/#entry-515627471806523)

## In Scott Duncan's words

He uses it in 192 posts, comments and articles. Where he says what it means, or uses it most plainly:

> You will never get paid. That's not what a Lien is for. A lien is always a CLAIM on an account that is Legal Estoppel.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 2, 26 May 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_ii/462493170453287_516706368365300/#entry-517335381635732)

> The ONLY reason to LIEN THE NAME is to SECURE it.
>
> The BIRTH CERTIFICATE is now YOURS. The TRUSTed party liening it, has endorsed this. It is now a FACT IN LAW (No legal authority exists, outside of yourself, to challenge it) that you own your name. Look at the relationship between you and your BANK when you have a MORTGAGE. It's the same thing.
>
> That's also means the TRUST has first rights of refusal, and that is LIMITED TO THE TERMS SET OUT IN IT.
>
> That's why NOBODY will give you credit. Until COMMERCE trusts your TRUST (Or the corporation holding it) you had better have value to sustain it.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 7, 22 April 2015](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_vii/462493170453287_714163491952919/#entry-820380601331207)

> Liening the NAME means the CORPORATION is presumed to have the value, not the name. The name OWES the corporation, so the name is responsible for the DEBT to the corporation, and nothing else. That's all the name DOES anymore...owe the corporation.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 2, 18 April 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_ii/462493170453287_498809460154991/#entry-504169786285625)
