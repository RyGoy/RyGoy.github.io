# Juridical Person

## Definitions

> A JURIDICAL PERSON is a LEGAL CONTRUCT. This may be a CORPORATION and/or a SECURITY and/or a TRUST. … The COURTS recognise some TRUSTS as “PRIVATE PERSONS”.
> — [PERSONS FOR IDIOTS, May 5, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/persons-for-idiots/)
