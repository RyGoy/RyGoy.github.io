# Veto

## Definitions

> Veto is the power to reject or block a particular course of action chosen by another. For example, in business law a veto prohibits an official action done by a company or firm. Further, a business can grant stockholders/shareholders the right to veto board decisions, thus stopping those decisions from coming to fruition.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/veto) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
