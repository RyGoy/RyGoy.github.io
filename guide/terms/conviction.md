# Conviction

## Definitions

> A conviction is an adjudication of a criminal defendant’s guilt; specifically, it is the act or judicial process of finding a criminal defendant guilty of a charged offense.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/conviction) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
