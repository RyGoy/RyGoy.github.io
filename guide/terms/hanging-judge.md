# Hanging Judge

## Definitions

> “Hanging Judge” refers to a judge who has abused his power and has been hung for the abuse of that trust. He gives sentences he should be hung for; HANGING JUDGE.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 2, May 30, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_ii/462493170453287_518715888164348/)
