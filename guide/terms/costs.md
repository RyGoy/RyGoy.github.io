# Costs

## Definitions

> Costs are the fees incurred for the use of a court and are seen in civil and criminal courts of all levels. Court costs usually include the initial filing fee, fees for serving the summons, complaint, and subpoenas, and fees to pay for the transcription by a court reporter of depositions or in-court testimony. Additionally, the cost of photocopying court papers and exhibits, providing daily small stipends to jurors, and paying the court clerk and the court marshal are often also included as applicable to the case.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/costs) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
