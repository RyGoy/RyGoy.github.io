# Withdraw

## Definitions

> WITHDRAW means they must file again, but they may use the same material.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 4, Dec 24, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_iv/462493170453287_603582623011007/#entry-603601639675772)

## In Scott Duncan's words

He uses it in 6 posts, comments and articles. Where he says what it means, or uses it most plainly:

> STAYED=They can just sign a piece of paper and it's real again. Similar to WITHDRAW. STAYED charges means the account is still "open" but dormant.
>
> WITHDRAW means they must file again, but they may use the same material.
>
> I won't give LEGAL advice, but I ALWAYS OBJECT when charges are STAYED or WITHDRAWN.
>
> DISMISSAL is the ONLY acceptable motion, as far as I'm concerned.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 4, 24 December 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_iv/462493170453287_603582623011007/#entry-603601639675772)

> You walk to the ATM and withdraw as much as you need...it's your account!
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, 5 April 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_462528253783112/#entry-500008946701709)
