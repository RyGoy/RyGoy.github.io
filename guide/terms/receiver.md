# Receiver

## Definitions

> The term receiver has two distinct legal meanings depending on context: In civil law, a receiver is a neutral person, often a professional fiduciary or trustee, appointed by a court to take custody of, manage, or preserve property that is the subject of litigation. The receiver acts as an officer of the court, not as an agent of any party, and exercises authority under the court’s supervision to prevent waste, fraud, or loss of the property in dispute. As stated by the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of North Carolina in SEC v. Elfindepan, S.A., 169 F. Supp. 2d 420, 426 (M.D.N.C.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/receiver) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
