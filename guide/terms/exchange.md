# Exchange

## Definitions

> Exchange refers both to the action of transferring goods and chattels for other goods and chattels of like value and to the transfer itself.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/exchange) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
