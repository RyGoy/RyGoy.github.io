# Agency

## Definitions

> Agency law is a common law doctrine controlling relationships between agents and principals. A principal-agent relationship is created when the agent is given authority to act on behalf of the principal. An agreement made by an agent is binding on the principal so long as the agreement was within the authority actually granted to the agent or reasonably perceived by a third party.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/agency) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
