# Tender

**Taught in:** [Tender, and discharge](../lens/08-tender-and-discharge.md)

## Definitions

> TENDER in the context of Law means, “to present to PERSON, an unconditional offer, to enter into a contract”.
> — [LEGALESE FOR IDIOTS, Jan 1, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/legalese-for-idiots/)

> TENDER = "CONDITIONALLY OPEN TO OFFERS"
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Mar 3, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_486919824677288/#entry-487001241335813)

> TENDER = UNCONDITIONAL ACCEPTANCE UPON USE
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 4, Nov 16, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_iv/462493170453287_588498674519402/#entry-588520927850510)

> TENDER = Unconditionally offered, with use being proof of acceptance.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 5, Feb 9, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_v/462493170453287_622715734431029/#entry-622716431097626)
