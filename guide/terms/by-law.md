# By-Law

## Definitions

> If it is a TRUST and not a PERSON WHO posts the NOTICE, it is what is known as a BY-LAW.  This means that should you choose (of your own free will) to interact with the TRUST, you are subject to the BY-LAWS.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Feb 19, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_482690341766903/)
