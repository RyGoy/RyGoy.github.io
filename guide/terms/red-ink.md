# Red Ink

## Definitions

> RED on a LEGAL DOCUMENT means there is a MISTAKE. NOTHING ELSE.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 7, Aug 14, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_vii/462493170453287_706971612672107/#entry-707011106001491)

> Red LEGALLY means there is a ERROR and it needs CORRECTING
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 8, Oct 1, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_viii/462493170453287_726731204029481/#entry-726837180685550)
