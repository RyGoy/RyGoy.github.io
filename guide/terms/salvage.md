# Salvage

## Definitions

> Salvage means the act of rescuing or saving endangered property or goods. For example, the act of saving a building from loss or unnecessary damage due to a fire. Salvage may also be used to refer to a salvage award, a monetary compensation given to a person who helps to save the lives of the crew, the cargo, or the ship from fire, capture, or otherwise, without having the legal duty to do it.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/salvage) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
