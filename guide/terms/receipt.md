# Receipt

## Definitions

> Receipt has two legal definitions: (1) A legal document evidencing a buyer has purchased and taken possession of the goods. A receipt can range from a small paper itemization of goods purchased in a retail setting to a document that a person storing an item has to prove another’s ownership (i.e. a warehouse receipt). (2) The act of receiving something. To illustrate, New York Tax Law § 1147 states that “[t]he mailing of such notice [as is required to be issued by the government under New York tax laws] shall be presumptive evidence of the receipt of the same by the person to whom addressed.”
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/receipt) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
