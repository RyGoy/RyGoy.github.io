# Marriage

## Definitions

> a PERFECTED, CONSENSUAL, CONSTRUCTIVE TRUST (MARRIAGE). There are three PARTIES in a TRUST, which means there are three PARTIES in a MARRIAGE.
> — [Obiter Dictum: MEADS V. MEADS FOR IDIOTS, May 5, 2014](https://thetenderforlaw.com/articles/scott-duncan/obiter-dictum-meades-v-meads-for-idiots/)

> no amount of love would compel her to JOINDER herself to a "BILL OF LADING", and hand it over to the GOVERNMENT. That means "getting married"
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Mar 11, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_490962517606352/)

> The marriage INCLUDES "Scott" AND "Tara", not "Scott" OR "Tara".  This means marriage makes you effectively ONE PERSON UNDER THE LAW. … A marriage ORGANIZES these "entities" into their respective roles.
> — [Scott Duncan, Volume 1, Mar 11, 2013](https://thetenderforlaw.com/vol_i/462493170453287_490962517606352/)
