# Advocate

## Definitions

> The term advocate functions as a noun and a verb. As a noun, an advocate (pronounced "ad-vuh-kit") is an individual who actively supports and promotes the interests of another person or enterprise. As a verb, to advocate (pronounced "ad-vuh-kate”) is the act of promoting the interest of another person or group.
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/advocate) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
