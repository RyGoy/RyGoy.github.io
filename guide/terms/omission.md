# Omission

## Definitions

> Omission is the failure to act or to disclose information when there is a responsibility to do so. See Brown v. Standard Casket Mfg. Co., 234 Ala. 512, 175 So. 358 (1937).
> — [Wex, Legal Information Institute, Cornell Law School](https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/omission) (Wex, Cornell's legal dictionary: not the archive's own words)
