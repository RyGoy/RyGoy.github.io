Niccolò Machiavelli (1469–1527) served the Florentine republic as a diplomat and official until the Medici returned to power in 1512. Out of office, he wrote *The Prince* in 1513 and dedicated it to Lorenzo de' Medici. It was published in 1532, after his death.

This copy is a PDF of the public-domain Project Gutenberg text of W. K. Marriott's translation, with his introduction. Gutenberg's edition also includes two shorter works by Machiavelli that Marriott translated: his account of how Cesare Borgia killed his rebellious captains, and his life of Castruccio Castracani (110 pages).
