Thomas Hobbes (1588–1679) published *Leviathan* in 1651. Its four parts cover human nature ("Of Man"), the making of the state by covenant ("Of Commonwealth"), a Christian commonwealth, and what he calls the "Kingdom of Darkness".

This copy is the 1914 Everyman's Library edition. It was scanned by the Digital Library of India from a copy in the State Central Library, Kolkata, and is held by the Internet Archive as a public-domain work. The file is about 35 MB.
