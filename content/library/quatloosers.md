[Quatloos](https://quatloos.com/) calls itself a public educational website about financial scams and frauds. Its forum has run threads on Scott Duncan since 2015, and files him with Robert Menard, Dean Clifford and the other "gurus" it follows. Its posters write under handles; its publisher, Quatloosia Publishing LLC, says their views aren't its own.

Scott made plain statements: what a tender is, what surety is, what the Charter governs, and what he is not. Here they are, beside what Quatloos said back. In the 659 posts of their main thread, and the others read for this page, no poster takes any of these statements and shows it to be wrong. The one time their moderator checked one, he agreed.

Every Quatloos post is linked, and "saved copy" beside each link opens a picture of the post as it stood in November 2026, in case it disappears.

### Tender

**Scott:** TENDER in the context of law means "to present to PERSON, an unconditional offer, to enter into a contract" ([Legalese for Idiots](/articles/scott-duncan/legalese-for-idiots/); [dictionary](/dictionary/#tender)).

**Quatloos:** Burnaby49, the forum's moderator, quotes that definition and declines to explain it {{< quatloos 195052 >}}. No poster offers a different definition, or says what is wrong with his.

### Surety

**Scott:** surety means the responsible party: surety is liability ([Surety for Idiots](/articles/scott-duncan/surety-for-idiots/); [dictionary](/dictionary/#surety)).

**Quatloos:** arayder says it is Robert Menard's "security of the person" theory again {{< quatloos 236999 >}}. They are different things: a surety is the party that answers for an obligation. Scott does speak of "security of the person", as your right to decide what happens to the PERSON you hold ([Persons for Idiots](/articles/scott-duncan/persons-for-idiots/)), but he calls Menard a fraud, and surety is his own word. arayder's summaries of what Scott teaches open with "as near as I can tell" {{< quatloos 236822 >}}, {{< quatloos 240315 >}}, and he wonders whether Scott took it from Karl Lentz, with no evidence {{< quatloos 240345 >}}.

### Measured against the OPCA article

The Library also holds Donald Netolitzky's [*Organized Pseudolegal Commercial Arguments as Magic and Ceremony*](/library/netolitzky-opca-magic-and-ceremony/) (Alberta Law Review, 2018), the closest thing to a field guide for what Quatloos says Scott is. Netolitzky credits Quatloos with "effective legal and historical criticism" of pseudolaw (p. 1085), so readers have reason to trust it. Here is what his article says pseudolaw is, beside what Scott actually teaches.

**The strawman.** Netolitzky's strawman is a second self, named in capitals and tied to you by your birth papers, that you cut away to escape the courts: "Get rid of the 'Strawman,' and you are free to do what you want" (p. 1070). Scott calls the word idiotic: "STRAWMAN = FREE-DUMB BULLSHIT" ([post](/vol_vi/462493170453287_671833389519263/#entry-672046449497957)), "a LOGICAL FALLACY" that "has NOTHING to do with LEGAL/LAW" ([post](/vol_xiii/462493170453287_960244817344784/#entry-960250877344178)), and members who used it were warned and banned ([post](/vol_vi/462493170453287_649151658454103/#entry-655442244491711)). What he teaches starts where Quebec's [Charter of human rights and freedoms](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/c-12?langCont=en) does: every human being "possesses juridical personality" (s. 1), a right the Charter ranks above any other Quebec statute (s. 52). The [Civil Code](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/fr/document/lc/ccq-1991?langCont=en) adds that you exercise your civil rights under the name in your act of birth (art. 5).

That juridical person is a legal agent: the piece each of us is given to play the board game of civilised society, and to keep its accounts. A stateless human, with none, can't take part at all. Netolitzky makes the same point against the strawman: a human with no legal person is not free but rightless, like a slave (p. 1075). Scott never tells anyone to discard theirs.

Between you and that PERSON, you are the agent and it is the principal. The birth certificate belongs to the government, and you are the only one authorised to act for the PERSON and bind it: "you are NOT the principal. You are merely AGENT" ([post](/vol_viii/462493170453287_734033383299263/#entry-750070815028853)). That is the ordinary law of mandate. An agent who acts within his authority, in the principal's name, is not personally liable (Civil Code, art. 2157); one who acts in his own name, or beyond his powers, is (arts. 2157–2158). Scott: "If the PRINCIPAL claims the AGENT acted without AUTHORIZATION, the AGENT MOST CERTAINLY has surety!" ([post](/vol_viii/462493170453287_731600073542594/#entry-786330924736175)). Nothing in this escapes the law. It asks which party the law holds responsible, which is the only thing the group concerns itself with: surety and accounting. Netolitzky mentions agency only as courtroom theatre (p. 1080).

**Silence means consent.** Netolitzky calls this the cornerstone error of pseudolaw: the idea that a notice, a fee schedule or a set of letters binds whoever fails to object (pp. 1049–50). He is right that an agreement can't be foisted on anyone. A court won't let a party drag another into costs on its own say-so, which is why the rules of civil procedure provide for security for costs. Scott says the same: "It's acquiescence. That is NOT an agreement. It's submission… Agreements have terms" ([post](/vol_ii/462493170453287_529112897124647/#entry-529131920456078)). When members proposed billing officials for every letter they opened, he answered "Stop scheduling for MONEY" ([post](/vol_iii/462493170453287_536001246435812/#entry-536023416433595)).

Where Scott parts from the OPCA litigant is the accounting. Whether you can set terms depends on whether you start from zero or hold the rights to value that someone else wants. A software licence is binding because the publisher owns the right being licensed, and its accounts show it; the same is true of anyone who holds the right. The OPCA litigant Netolitzky describes never does that accounting: he assumes he is owed something, and never asks whether he gave up his rights in an earlier transaction. Scott's question is always the reverse: who holds the surety, and what do the books show?

For Scott, silence is acquiescence, not agreement. Contracts are about our rights and duties toward each other, and working with others takes communication. So silence counts where it was not prudent to stay silent: you asked for clarification and got none, or someone owed you an answer and kept quiet. Even then it is acquiescence, not a contract with terms. That has a footing in ordinary law: a trustee who fails to account and inform, and stays silent, may be in breach of his fiduciary duty. And the fee schedule, on his account, collects nothing. "These 'fees' are never paid. they are simply MORE DEBT" ([post](/vol_i/462493170453287_495891060446831/#entry-495994400436497)); their only effect is to void the instrument, like stamping VOID on a cheque, so "NOBODY can benefit" ([post](/vol_iii/462493170453287_536001246435812/#entry-536025163100087)).

**Income tax.** Netolitzky lists avoiding income tax first among the aims of OPCA litigation (p. 1048). Scott's position is accounting, not avoidance: tax falls on income, and for a business, the liability exists only where revenue exceeds expenses. A deposit is not in itself income; a loan, a gift or a transfer between your own accounts is not taxed. "I pay my taxes," he wrote ([Free-Dumb: The Truth & Robert Menard](/articles/scott-duncan/freedumb-the-truth-and-robert-menard/)), and he files every quarter, as Quatloos itself quotes him {{< quatloos 254225 >}}. His returns show zero. Burnaby49 answers there that a zero return counts as not filing. It doesn't: a return is a return, and an individual who owes no tax for the year isn't required to file one at all, unless he disposed of capital property or the CRA demands it ([Income Tax Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/I-3.3/section-150.html), s. 150(1.1)(b) and (2)). That is the opposite of the OPCA litigant, who claims to owe nothing and never keeps the books to show it. Accounting is half of LEGAL.

**Freemen and Menard.** Netolitzky names Robert Menard as the guru who brought the strawman and the Freeman-on-the-Land movement to Canada (pp. 1069–70), and Quatloos files Scott beside him. Scott's view of Menard, held from 2013 on, is precise: Menard tells the truth about the law, and lies through omission. "Everything Robert Menard says about the law is true. It's what he doesn't say that's the lie" ([post](/vol_ii/462493170453287_518715888164348/)). The omission is always the same: what "THIS NOTE IS LEGAL TENDER" means. Menard teaches that statutes bind you only by consent, so you can opt out, while living on the legal tender that carries those statutes with it. Scott's answer is the accounting: "If you BENEFIT, then you have OBLIGATION!" Same facts, opposite conclusion. Menard tells you that you are free; Scott tells you what you owe, and to whom.

Scott uses "freeman" too, but as a legal title, not an escape: "Freemen is a LEGAL TITLE. That's why Menard uses the fraudulent 'Freeman-on-the-land' bullshit" ([post](/vol_vi/462493170453287_674188432617092/#entry-694901187212483)). And he draws the line Netolitzky draws around gurus, money: "People who charge MONEY for LEGAL KNOWLEDGE are in fact ethical scum. Shrout and Menard are the same kind of scum" ([post](/vol_vii/462493170453287_695326923836576/#entry-696767613692507)). Scott never charged, and he could say "NO VICTIMS" ([post](/vol_x/462493170453287_813678212001446/#entry-815231931846074)). In 2017 he named what Quatloos would do: "The FIRST thing the Quatlosers do is try to lump us in with the Free-Dumbers like Robert-Arthur: Menard and Dean C. Clifford" ([post](/vol_xvi/462493170453287_1160275230675074/#entry-1240645799304683)).

**Escaping the courts.** Netolitzky's OPCA litigant claims "extraordinary authority and immunity" (p. 1045). Scott's position is the opposite: the courts are for accounting, and the PERSON is surety. It can be compelled to appear, above all where there is an injured party with a legitimate claim. What Scott warns against is joinder: the man stepping into the principal's place and taking on its surety himself. "I do not consent to joinder with the GOVERNMENT" ([Joinder for Idiots](/articles/scott-duncan/joinder-for-idiots/)). That is why he told members facing a charge not to answer to the name: say "I am here against my will, and I wish to leave", not "Ok, I'll be your bitch and assume surety if you let me go" ([post](/vol_iv/462493170453287_581196165249653/#entry-583703278332275)). It is the rule of mandate again: an agent who acts in his own name becomes personally liable (art. 2157). His [Notice of Mistake](/articles/scott-duncan/notice-of-mistake/) doesn't deny the court either. It asks a question: "WHAT EVIDENCE does the COURT have that I… have any SURETY in this matter?"

Liening your name works like a mortgage on a house: "When a bank mortgages a property for you, it places a LIEN on that property." It is "a PUBLIC RECORD of an undisputed CLAIM" ([Lien Your Name](/articles/scott-duncan/lien-your-name/)). The courts make money with the legal name, all of the value is credit, and a lien stops the creation of new credit against the underlying asset. It is no escape: "your obligation is to the principal claim." And it costs: "Nobody will give you credit otherwise… If you have no VALUE, you get no CREDIT." Nor is it permission to break contracts. In the same post he attacks "the very stupid position that ignoring CONTRACTS, breaking promises, and just plain committing FRAUD, is OK… There is NOTICE. … Don't like the obligations that come with 'Legal Tender'? DON'T USE IT" ([post](/vol_xi/462493170453287_851550111547589/)).

"I still wield more AUTHORITY than any COURT does," he wrote, but in the same breath: "I never claimed to be 'SOVEREIGN'" ([Meads v. Meads for Idiots](/articles/scott-duncan/obiter-dictum-meades-v-meads-for-idiots/)). The authority he meant was over his own crews, on his own vessels, "because a bunch of people with boats TRUST me."

**Money for nothing.** Netolitzky's debt-elimination schemes pay a creditor with paper that is worth nothing, such as homemade bonds and "WeRe Cheques", so the debtor keeps the value (p. 1056). Scott's rule runs the other way: "If you get a BILL or a SIGNED INVOICE, then pay it" ([post](/vol_i/462493170453287_480677688634835/#entry-481537738548830)). On Accepted for Value, the best-known of those schemes: "Fuck the Freeman/a4v shit! They want money GIVE IT TO THEM" ([post](/vol_i/462493170453287_484745448228059/#entry-484817881554149)). And of himself: "Banks lend me money all the time, because I PAY IT BACK" ([post](/vol_xii/462493170453287_912484872120779/#entry-913624138673519)).

Scott's starting point is that money is debt: it is created against a promise to repay, a promise that may or may not be kept. Even the Bank of England explains that most money comes into being when banks make loans ([2014](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/quarterly-bulletin/2014/q1/money-creation-in-the-modern-economy)). That risk is why sureties exist at all: someone has to answer if the promise fails. Netolitzky never asks who that is. His article never uses the word "accounting" or "surety", and never considers that an agent is not liable for his principal's debt.

When Scott wrote that "PAYMENT is actually ILLEGAL," he was drawing an accounting distinction, not offering a way out: fiat currency doesn't pay a debt, it discharges it. "The ONLY alternative is either to set it off on the books, or discharge the debt" ([post](/vol_i/462493170453287_483058458396758/#entry-483199968382607)). Discharge is "to 'zero-out' a balance of an item on an account" ([post](/vol_iii/462493170453287_532937380075532/#entry-532943700074900)). Either way the debt is accounted for. Even the power to create money that a lien gives comes with the opposite of a windfall: "YOU CANNOT BENEFIT FROM EVEN ONCE CENT of what you create. An OWNER cannot be a beneficiary, EVER, and the bookkeeping requirements are INSANE" ([post](/vol_iv/462493170453287_573236309378972/#entry-872645652771368)).
