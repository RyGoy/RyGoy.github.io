Isaac Asimov (1920–1992) wrote the Foundation stories in the 1940s, and they were gathered as *Foundation* in 1951, the first book of the trilogy. Seldon's Foundation is set up to preserve knowledge and steer a thousand years of history. Each crisis it meets is settled less by force than by trade, religion and the control of technology.

The second Foundation book: the Foundation survives an attack by the last great general of the dying Empire, then faces the Mule, a mutant who can bend people's emotions and whom Hari Seldon's plan did not foresee.

The last book of the Foundation trilogy: the Mule, and then the Foundation itself, hunt for the hidden Second Foundation that Hari Seldon set up to keep his plan on course.
