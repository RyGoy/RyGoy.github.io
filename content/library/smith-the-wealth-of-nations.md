Adam Smith (1723–1790) was a Scottish moral philosopher. *The Wealth of Nations*, published in London in 1776, is in five books: on the division of labour and prices; on capital; on the progress of wealth in different nations; on systems of political economy; and on the revenue of the sovereign or state.
