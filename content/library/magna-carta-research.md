The Magna Carta Project was a research project for the Charter's 800th anniversary in 2015, funded by the Arts and Humanities Research Council. Its site gives the text of Magna Carta 1215 with a translation and an expert commentary on each clause, so you can read what was granted, by whom, and to whom, in the words of the charter itself.

A few places to start, all on the Project's site:

- [The 1215 Magna Carta](https://magnacartaresearch.org/read/magna_carta_1215), clause by clause
- [The Articles of the Barons](https://magnacartaresearch.org/read/articles_of_barons), the demands agreed before the charter was sealed
- [The copies of Magna Carta](https://magnacartaresearch.org/read/magna_carta_copies), the originals that survive and where they are kept
- [The itinerary of King John](https://magnacartaresearch.org/read/itinerary), where the King was, day by day
- [The original charters of King John](https://magnacartaresearch.org/read/original_charters)

The site calls itself work in progress, and its material may change.
