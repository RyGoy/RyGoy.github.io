Bible Hub puts many translations of the same verse on one page, so you can see where they agree and where they part ways. It also has an interlinear that sets the Hebrew and Greek under the English, Strong's concordance for looking up each original word, and commentaries old and new.

A few places to start, all on Bible Hub:

- [Genesis 1 in the King James Version](https://biblehub.com/kjv/genesis/1.htm)
- [Genesis 1 in the Latin Vulgate](https://biblehub.com/vul/genesis/1.htm) (the Clementine edition of 1592)
- [The Hebrew and Greek interlinear](https://biblehub.com/interlinear/)
- [Strong's concordance](https://biblehub.com/strongs.htm)

Bible Hub follows the 66 books of the Protestant canon. For the books only some churches keep, such as Tobit, Sirach and the Maccabees in Catholic and Orthodox Bibles, or 1 Enoch, Jubilees and Meqabyan in the Ethiopian Bible, you will need other translations.

For how the Ethiopian Bible, the Latin Vulgate and the King James Bible differ, and a list of other Bibles to compare, see [Which Bible?](/library/#which-bible) on the Library page.
