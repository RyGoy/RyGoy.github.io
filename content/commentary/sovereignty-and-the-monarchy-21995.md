These are not the same thing, the Founding Fathers of America made the mistake of discarding the monarchy instead of consciously crafting a version of their own. More than 200 years later the role of a proper [English style Monarch](https://thetenderforlaw.com/library/the-english-constitution/) is needed, and no one knows why. That is what this writing aims to explain.

In 17th century France, the King is sovereign (owns title to all land) and rules by divine right (authority from God), in 13th century England, this  tradition was being modified for the benefit of common people and it starts with the [Magna Carta](https://thetenderforlaw.com/library/magna-carta-research/). It is a fact that Christian Monarchs derived authority from Rome, the kingdoms of England, France, Scotland were all Papal fiefs. The original Magna Carta was voided by the Pope himself and King John had even transferred title of all his lands to the Church before Runnymede ever happened. Few know the role the Knights Templar played in assuring the stability that a monarch would provide in such a dispute, but it’s worth looking into if you take Western style constitutions seriously. By the 17th century in England we had cemented the idea of popular sovereignty in the roots of our culture. The cost was 400,000 pounds, the head of Charles I, and a half century of political instability until the Monarchy of William AND Mary. English Monarchy works, and we fought long and hard to forge it into the tool it is today.

The American Constitution is still the best in the world, but it’s not perfect, it made a Monarch out of the President in the eyes of the People but gave him more power and less responsibility, the English Monarch since Charles I MUST sign his/her own death warrant if laid in front of them by the Houses, the President has no such obligation.

Canada has it’s own problems, we are in no position to judge our older brother, they had a fight with the parents at a young age and moved out on their own, we’re like the 40 year old that still lives in the basement… but it’s so much worse, let me explain:

1. [Canada never confederated](https://thetenderforlaw.com/library/ho-canada/), you would have to change the definition of confederation to say that the Dominion was a confederation. Canada’s colonies united as a Dominion under the Crown until the Dominion ended in 1931, the purpose of the Dominion was to pay war debt Britain owed the United States for Canada’s involvement in aiding the South during the American Civil War.

2. The Stature of Westminster ended the Dominion and the Office of the Governor General. Canada’s Government is DE FACTO for this reason, see the [1947 Letters Patent](https://thetenderforlaw.com/library/letters-patent-1947/) and look for SURETY like Scott taught us.

The only British institution that existed to “act” as a trustee was the Law Society of Upper Canada, they quickly transferred surety to the Bank of Canada and started “managing” the title that few Canadians ever bothered claiming.
