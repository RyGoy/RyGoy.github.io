GTA VI got a 99 on Metacritic. Every critic agreed. When every expert agrees, check who's paying the experts.

## 30 frames per second

Thirteen years after GTA V. Cuck simulator at 30fps, no toxic masculinity allowed. Broken in game physics, trains that won't run you over... was this game made in India?

Nobody asked for a 30fps Cuck simulator. Somebody made this decision. Accountants set policy.

**Grand Fluff Auto:** your frame rate is between you and your graphics card.

![Cover art: "Grand Theft Masculinity", a bearded man with crossed arms in front of a Vice City sunset](/images/grand-fluff-auto/grand-theft-masculinity.jpg)

## The opening

Reviewers say the first chapters are mostly Jason and Lucia's home life. You paid $80 to watch a couple argue about bills. You could do that at home for free. You probably did.

**Grand Fluff Auto:** you spawn, you steal a car. Your home life is your own business.

![A children's-book cover: "Franklin Finds the Bread Stale and the Circus Underwhelming". The turtle sits holding a controller while GTA 6 on the TV says TASK: SWEEP THE FLOOR](/images/grand-fluff-auto/franklin-bread-stale.jpg "Bread and circuses, minus the circus.")

## The missions

Critics found them bossy: go here, stand there, do this. Women think there is a male lonliness epidemic, but they just don't seem to know if they're being a cunt.

**Grand Fluff Auto:** nobody tells you what to do. If they try, ask for the contract.

![Cover art: "LBGTA6, Beta Cuck Edition", the cast in front of a rainbow Vice City skyline](/images/grand-fluff-auto/lbgta6.jpg)

## The price

$79.99. $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, which is the same game plus the feeling of having paid $99.99.

**Grand Fluff Auto:** free with your licensed copy of GTA V on Steam.

![Fry from Futurama holding out a fistful of cash: "shut up and give me my money back"](/images/grand-fluff-auto/give-me-my-money-back.jpg)

## Launch day

Sign-in failures. Crashes in the prologue. A mission that locks your progress. People falling through the ground.

**Grand Fluff Auto:** the ground is from 2013. It has held for thirteen years. Build a house on it.

**Grand Fluff Auto:** there is only the PC version.
