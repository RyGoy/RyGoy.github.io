Each card describes a transaction. Mark the account or accounts to debit and to credit, then check your answer. Amounts are given for context; you only choose accounts.

Unless a card says otherwise, assume a corporation, a perpetual inventory system, the allowance method for bad debts, and the gross method for discounts.
