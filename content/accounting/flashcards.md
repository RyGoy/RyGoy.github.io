Each card describes a transaction. Mark the account or accounts to debit and to credit, then check your answer.

**New to accounting?** Read [Start Here: Debits and Credits](/accounting/start-here/) first; it takes five minutes. The game opens on Mia's Lemonade Stand, twelve beginner cards played in order, with the stand's books under the card showing each answer written in. Then it moves you on to the company decks.

In the company decks, amounts are given for context; you only choose accounts. Unless a card says otherwise, assume a corporation, a perpetual inventory system, the allowance method for bad debts, and the gross method for discounts.
