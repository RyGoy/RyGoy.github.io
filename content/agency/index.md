## The agency test

An agency relationship exists when all three of these are true:

1. **Agreement**: the principal and the agent agree that the agent will act for the principal.
2. **On the principal's behalf**: the agent acts for the principal, not for themselves.
3. **Control**: the agent acts subject to the principal's control.

When all three are present, the principal can be held legally responsible for what the agent does. The Restatement (Third) of Agency, § 1.01, defines agency the same way.
