Remember the day Dean was Arrested? Lou Manotti went off to the seminar (NO he DIDN'T PAY!) to chat with Dean Clifford, but ended up making sure Norah Holloway didn't get swept up in the mayhem...

...he's really heroic when it comes to women, but the guy will totally bail the helm, when we are careening toward a million-dollar yacht. (Save us Admiral!) :D

Anyway I announced the seminar a THE PLACE TO GO if you wanted to avoid me. Aquilae had 2 OFFICERS there that you DON'T know too, so even though I wasn't there... I was there.

But THE NAME OF THE DOCTOR was playing that day, so I thought to myself, "Fuck their rights and personal liberties! They'll still be stupid after, and I know what's important"!

So, now that the "shock" has died down, you might want to experience that day from my perspective :D
