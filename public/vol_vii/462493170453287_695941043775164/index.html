"Toronto constitutional lawyer Rocco Galati said the blanket order shrouding the vast majority of investigations into complaints against judges is “an abuse and excess of power” and is an example of “judges protecting judges.” Galati...said allowing secrecy in the courts carries serious consequences.

“In the darkness of secrecy, sinister interest and evil in every shape have full swing,” he said, quoting 18th-century British jurist and philosopher Jeremy Bentham. “Where there is no publicity there is no justice.”
