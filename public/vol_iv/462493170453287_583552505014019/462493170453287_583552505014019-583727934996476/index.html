Found it! Here is what David taught on public notices:

"There are Three Types of Notice: 1. Legal Notice which is County-Wide - that is Found within the LEGAL NOTICE Classified Section of Every County-Seat Newspaper; Every Attorney Reads that LEGAL NOTICE Section EVERY Day!!! 2. Public Notice which is World-Wide - that is Made By a Recording With the County Recorder Office; 3. Actual Notice which is Specific - that is Made By Service Using Certified Mail and is Personal.... I suppose that there exists other examples - such as I tell you to stop and you fail to take notice and I drop you were you don't under-Stand,,,,, For the Purposes of the Estate, the Executor has been Absent or AOL Since Attaining Age of Majority or twenty-one years,,,, Now Suddenly Someone [the World Knows Who They Are] Appears and Claims To Occupy the Executor Office.... Has There Been Notice Given? Which is Due-Process of Law.... We Suggest that you Folk's Seeking To Fill and Function Within the Executor or Executrix Office Do The Following: Publish the following in the LEGAL NOTICE Section of the County-Seat Newspaper [and if you have to ask what or which that is - You Are NOT Competent to do this]; I [David Clarence Schroll] am Executor to the DAVID CLARENCE SCHROLL [Note four spaces] Estate as Witnessed by my Sole Ability to Personally Obtain a CERTIFICATE OF BIRTH and Do Hereby Give Legal, Lawful, Public and Actual Notice of The Same - Hereby and Herein. By: executor David Clarence Schroll, of my own right. nation Pennsylvania. general post-office. county York. Schroll Province. United States Minor Outlying Islands. Normally a Legal Notice must appear in the county seat newspaper Once per Week for Three Weeks - Then it is Considered to Be a Fact. Then the Newspaper Will Provide a Certification of the Publication; Take that Certification and Record it with the County Recorder; When the Recorder Returns the Original Marked, Stamped and Recorded - "Re-Publish" that Recording in the County-Seat Newspaper worded like this: This is "Re-Notice" that the former Legal Notice given by Publication [Insert the Publication dates here] in this Newspaper - has been Duly Recorded with the [York County Recorder Book [XXX] and Page [XXXX] and is Now and Forever Legal, Lawful, Public and Actual Notice of The Same - Hereby and Herein. By: executor David Clarence Schroll, of my own right. nation Pennsylvania. general post-office/ county York. Schroll Province. United States Minor Outlying Islands. Now - When you Obtain the Certification from that Newspaper - Take that Certification and "Re-Record" it with the County Recorder; AND Attach One-Dollar To It As Previously Instructed [and if you weren't paying attention then you are NOT Competent To Do This]. When that is completed - Obtain a Certified Copy of This Recording and Photo-copy AND the First Recording and Serve those copies upon the following: Governor Office Birth State; and Governor Office of Present State if Not Living on Birth State; Secretary of State Office Birth State; and Secretary of State Office of Present State if Not Living on Birth State; Treasurer Office Birth State; and Treasurer Office of Present State if Not Living on Birth State; Attorney General Office Birth State; and Attorney General Office of Present State if Not Living on Birth State; United States Secretary of State Office; United States Office of the Treasury; United States Office of the Department of the Treasury; There may be other Offices that should be notified such as the Bureau of Census and Department of Commerce, you may do so if you'd like.... Use CERTIFIED MAIL for each mailing as We Showed You How To Do - on a Cover Letter to Each of Those Offices, using the Executor Office Letter Format [if You can't figure out how to do this you are NOT Competent To Do So]. A copy of the CERTIFIED COPY of the ORIGINAL Recording and Final Re-Recording is to Be Attached "by" paper-clip to Every Executor Letter that You Serve From that Time Foreword and in the Person of the Executor Office Before All BAR-Fly Tribunals and to the Notice of Mailing Location Change to General Post-Office.... Now Conduct Yourself Competently Within the Executor or Executrix Office and - Go and Sin No More.... Praise Your Yahweh Father For His Blessing's,,,,,, You are Less that Twenty-Four Month's From His Judgement "12-21-12" = 333"

Cipher though the Yahweh horseshit and use what you can use if your goal is to make proper public notice regarding the office of executor.
