I have not received anything from my Dad’s estate as per his will. They have sold the house, emptied all the bank accounts and liquidated all of his (their co-owned) assets.

Essentially, their lawyer says that his will is worthless and be entirely ignored due to “rights of survivorship” which says that the surviving spouse gets all the deceased spouses assets.

After almost a year of trying to get them to recognise the will and provide me with bank statements etc so I can settle the estate, they will not even respond to my letters, phone calls etc.

Legally, I am responsible to settle the estate as per his final wishes. Also, I am obligated to do his final taxes (including estate taxes etc). I canât do this because I cannot obtain any of the info that I need.

I want to charge them criminally, but I’m not sure what acts and statutes, criminal code sections I should reference when I inform a JP and submit an application to charge her criminally.

I’m desperate at this point and I don’t know what sections of statutes etc to cite.
