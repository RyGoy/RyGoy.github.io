The officer conducted a traffic stop and spoke with the male driver of the vehicle who refused to identify himself and failed to provide ID.

      As a result of the investigation Dean Russell Kory, 48, of Antrim Street, Peterborough, was arrested and charged with obstruct a peace officer.       The accused is also facing two charges under the Compulsory Automobile Insurance Act.
