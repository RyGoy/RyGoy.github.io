Just for fun, I decided to look up my hometown courthouse on Dun and Bradstreet. I was having some difficulty locating it under the expected name, "Norfolk County Courthouse", so I turned to Google. My search results turned up this:

https://www.google.ca/#q=norfolk+county+courthouse%2C+simcoe%2C+ontario+

How interesting. The top result is "Ministry of the Attorney General; courthouse." Okay, let's punch that into dnb.ca and see what we get:

https://www.dnbexpress.ca/search_results.asp

When I was last in this courthouse back in 2012, the listing was "Government of Ontario, Operating As: Justice." Now it's "Regional Health Authority - Central Manitoba Inc, Operating As: Minister Of The Attorney General"
