      I had Timmies for lunch and then I went down to provincal court to have my affidavit of Notice of Mistake notarized, again. (new and improved). The clerk asked me what it is for? So I asked her to do it..she seemed uncertain...but she did it after. But....it if she didn't, would it have be a notarized document? I requested a certified true copy of my probation order and bail recognizance. Was told it takes 7-10 days. Will mail to me.
