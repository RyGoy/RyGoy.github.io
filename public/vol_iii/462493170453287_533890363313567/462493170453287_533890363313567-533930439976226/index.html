As I plan these things with machines, I need certain events to come to pass, before I execute the next steps in my larger plan.

As I said. I mean to do some damage. Derek Hill is the first to demonstrate TRUE UNDERSTANDING of what I say.

This means approximately 10% (+ or - 3%) of readers TRULY understand NOTICE OF MISTAKE. Now I close the group.

The rest of you have to catch up to Derek (I STILL can't believe I am saying that, and telling the truth) in your UNDERSTANDING in what I say. As you do this, I will be teaching you more about MONEY. This will be essential, later.

THE PLAN:

Only members can see posts now. Because it's a LARGE group, this is a ONE-WAY TRIP.

Then I shall go on a "cleaning spree".

I will hunt down, and weed out the Theists. I will hunt down, and weed out the AGENTS

All the while waiting for the next one of you to trigger the next step.

And because it's me...

SPOILER ALERT: ALL of you have my attention now. I hope I have yours, because if I am successful in my plan, I will enslave every person here. Every person here will serve me, and they will do so of their own free will. I will take all of your value, and return it tenfold. You see, to serve me, is to follow your slave mandate. Learn how to be FREE.

I'm better than God. I really am, because I can't stand the sight of all of you on your knees. I sickens me that you are robbed of your labour, and skill, and time, and CHILDREN. If you MUST be a slave, labour to be MY slave.

I need to build a fleet.

I need to crew my vessels.

I need people on land who can do stuff for us. Build stuff for us. SELL stuff for us.

I will train my slaves for occupations that don't even exist yet, and deploy them to serve US. They will have to suffer getting wealthy in the process, or having a larger purpose. Above all, my slaves won't think wrong. They will NOT value the wrong things.

...and they will truly know what they want out of this one live that's being stolen from them.

ALL of you have skills. I require them, and I intend to use them for personal gain. Fortunately that involves all of you prospering.

You won't follow my orders because you have "faith" in me. You won't follow my orders because you "believe" in me...or even TRUST me.

You won't follow my orders because I have "Rank", or "Title" or even "Authority".

You will follow my orders because you will instantly know why I gave them, and how fucking awesomely clever I am.

But that's just for those who make it.

People can leave if the "creepy Nobility Admiral guy" who appears to be rounding up slaves, is ringing your "this guy's nuts" radar... Go. Thanks for your time. Better people than you have suggested some sort of mental frailty, yet I appear to be ALWAYS RIGHT. Make of that what you will. Enslaving people is in my blood, but I can't own slaves and look at the guy in the mirror... and I really like that guy. He does some awesome shit.

Imagine a navy FILLED with "Judges" :D We will build SAFE HARBOUR for those seeking sanctuary.
