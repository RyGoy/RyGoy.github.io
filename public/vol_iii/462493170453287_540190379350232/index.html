To regain their vessel, they had to APPEAR in the FLESH and CLAIM/SWEAR that they were ALIVE and well and therefore were able (COMPETENT) to manage their own ESTATE. In this way, the masses became slaves by the presumption of their consent, and their ignorance of this particular Tender For Law.
