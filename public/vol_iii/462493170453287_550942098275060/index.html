Something I've been pondering regarding the accounting aspect of the legal system...

Am I right in saying that when one is arrested and charged it is the Police's corporation that create the account and seek to discharge?

Can a random PERSON seek an action against another for say, breaking a red light? Or would the incident have to be reported to the proper authorities and they then seek to discharge the account? Pat on the back for the informant...

I'd imagine the courts would be a little more thorough when determining valid grounds for a case when it is one man (through his person obviously) seeking an action against another i.e. contract/injury as opposed to when the authorities rely on ignorance and coersion...
