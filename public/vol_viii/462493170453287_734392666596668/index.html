Receipt of, and, or, response to a letter or any communication initiated by you, and, or Samford and Sons: $5,000.00 CDN Receipt of a Telephone Call: $5,000.00 CDN Phone Message to Bernard Carroll or me: $5,000.00 CDN Receipt of a court action initiated by Tom Kerekes, and or you and, or, Samford and Sons: $25,000.00 CDN
